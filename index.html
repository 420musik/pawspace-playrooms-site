Sanctuary FAQ · Pre-Launch Guide

Everything Included in Every Private Session

UK Solo Playrooms
When does PawSpace open for live bookings?
We are completing our final pre-launch preparations and are not ready for live bookings just yet. Our booking system will become available as soon as we open. Right now, you can save your dog profile, preview 60, 90, and 120-minute time slots, and chat with our AI Receptionist.
Are reactive dogs and exempt XL Bullies welcome?
Yes. Every session guarantees 100% private solo occupancy with zero external dogs, 6ft anti-dig perimeter fencing, double-gated airlock entry, and a 5-minute changeover buffer. Exempt XL Bullies are warmly welcomed with a valid UK Certificate of Exemption, third-party insurance, and lead & muzzle until securely locked inside.
How does contactless smart gate PIN access work?
On Opening Day, every confirmed reservation generates a unique time-bound 4-digit keypad PIN sent via SMS and displayed in My Key, giving you and your dog sole contactless entry for your private indoor playroom session.
What are the session prices, promos, and 30% loyalty reward?
Sessions are £28.00 for 60 minutes (£23.00 with code PAWSPACE or £20.00 with FIRSTVISIT), £38.00 for 90 minutes, and £48.00 for 120 minutes. Completing 5 paid sessions automatically unlocks 30% OFF your 6th session with LOYALTY30.
